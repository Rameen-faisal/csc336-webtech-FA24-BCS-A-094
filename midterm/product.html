Graphic Print T-Shirt

Rs. 1,990

Description

A soft, breathable 100% cotton t-shirt with a bold front graphic print. Regular fit with a ribbed crew neck, made for everyday comfort.

The fabric is pre-washed to keep its shape and softness, wash after wash. The print is applied with a water-based ink that stays vivid and does not crack or peel, so the design looks as good on the hundredth wear as it did on the first.

The regular cut sits comfortably on the shoulders and falls straight through the body, which makes it easy to wear on its own in warm weather or layered under a jacket or hoodie when it gets cooler. The ribbed neckline is double stitched so it holds its shape and does not stretch out.

Care instructions: machine wash cold with similar colours, turn the t-shirt inside out to protect the print, tumble dry low, and do not bleach. Iron on low heat on the reverse side only, and never iron directly over the print.

Sizing: available in S, M, L and XL. If you are between two sizes we recommend choosing the larger one for a relaxed fit. The model in the photo is 6 ft tall and is wearing a size M.

Shipping and returns: orders are packed within one working day and delivered in three to five working days. Unworn items with tags attached can be returned within fourteen days of delivery for an exchange or a refund.

Styling ideas: pair it with slim fit denim jeans and canvas sneakers for an easy everyday look, or throw a denim jacket over it for the evening. The neutral base and bold print also work well with plain shorts during summer, so it earns its place in almost any wardrobe.

Quality promise: every piece is inspected by hand before it leaves our warehouse, checking the stitching, the print alignment and the fabric finish, so what arrives at your door is exactly what you saw on the page.
